A story is a narrative set in the unit's world, with its cast, a problem and an ending.

1. Follow the premise: who, where, what happens, how it ends.
2. Use the patterns the plan requires and keep to the share of known patterns the recipe sets.
3. Teach only the table words the plan lists, each used where the story needs it.
4. Keep to the length the plan gives for one part.
5. Write comprehension questions that can be answered from the text, on the points the plan names.
