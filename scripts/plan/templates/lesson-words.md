A word lesson teaches the words of one topic inside one situation. Its patterns are already known, so every new word goes straight into sentences the learner can build.

1. Give each new word a card: the word, its meaning, and an example sentence made of known words and this lesson's words.
2. Order the cards so that related words sit together and each example can lean on the cards before it. A word card keeps one example (`example_fr`, `example_en`); a concept card that shows how the topic's words are used holds its examples as a list (see Concept cards below), such as `{ "fr": "J'ai trois chats.", "en": "I have three cats." }`.
3. For each new verb, give its table when it follows no pattern the learner has.
4. Build the items around what the learner can now do in the situation: ask, answer, describe, choose. At least half are set in the situation.
5. Carry words from the last lessons into the items, the words due for reuse first.
