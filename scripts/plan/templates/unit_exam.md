A unit exam teaches nothing. It samples the unit to show the learner is ready for the next one, in one sitting.

1. Write the number of items per lesson goal the plan gives, interleaved, with a few items that bring back earlier units where the plan asks for them.
2. Keep to the share of production items the recipe sets.
3. No item repeats, or nearly repeats, an item of a lesson or of the practice set.
4. Each item names the goal it checks.
5. Slang and other words marked for recognition are tested for understanding only.
