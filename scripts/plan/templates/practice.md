A practice set teaches nothing. It gives the learner more of what the unit's lessons taught, mixed together.

1. Group the items by the lesson goals of the unit and interleave them, so no two neighbours practise the same goal.
2. Set the items in the unit's situations and reuse the reading's table words.
3. No item repeats, or nearly repeats, an item of a lesson.
4. Slang and other words marked for recognition are asked for their meaning and who would say them, never produced.
