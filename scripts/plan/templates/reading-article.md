An article is a short piece of non-fiction on a custom, a piece of history or a place: what a newspaper or magazine would print.

1. Open with what the piece is about, then give the facts in an order a reader can follow.
2. Every fact is checked against a source before it is written. Leave out a date or detail you cannot confirm.
3. People may be quoted or described, but the piece informs; it does not tell a story.
4. Use the patterns the plan requires, teach only the table words the plan lists, and keep to the length of one part.
5. Write comprehension questions on what the article says and why.
